About Pauli

Pauli Hartmann

Licensed Cosmetologist · Makeup Artist · Somerset County, NJ

With over 15 years of experience in the beauty industry, Pauli Hartmann brings salon-quality expertise directly to your door. Trained and certified in a wide range of beauty disciplines, Pauli has built a loyal clientele across Somerset County through her meticulous attention to detail, warm personality, and commitment to making every client feel confident and beautiful.

Whether it's a flawless bridal look, a fresh color treatment, or a relaxing in-home facial, Pauli approaches every appointment with professionalism and genuine care. Her mobile service means you enjoy the luxury of a beauty experience on your schedule, in your own space.
